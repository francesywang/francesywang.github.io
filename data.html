Chinese Media Campaigns

Chinese Media Campaigns on Territorial Disputes (CMCTD) — relevant People’s Daily articles during twenty-one Chinese territorial crises, 1946–2017.

Chinese Media Campaigns Opponent-Referenced (CMCOR) — context windows around references to opponent countries and governments.

Survey Experiment Data

A survey experiment of 7,830 respondents in China, Vietnam, and the Philippines, fielded March 10–April 4, 2021.

Chinese Diplomatic Tweets

With Stephanie Kang: 143,048 tweets from 150 unique Chinese diplomatic Twitter accounts, January 2017–January 2021.

Qualitative Archival Data

Archival materials on the South China Sea dispute and the Sino-Vietnamese border conflict from collections in China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Vietnam.
