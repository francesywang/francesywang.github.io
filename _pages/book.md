### *China’s Strategic Use of Media in Interstate Disputes*

- Honorable Mention, ISA International Security Studies Section (ISSS) Best Book Award

## Overview

*The Art of State Persuasion* investigates how China uses state media as a tool of foreign policy during international conflicts. It examines why certain disputes are amplified while others are downplayed and how propaganda strategy can create political room for either mobilization or restraint.
