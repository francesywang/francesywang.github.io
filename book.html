Overview

The Art of State Persuasion examines China’s strategic use of state propaganda during international crises, explaining why certain disputes are amplified while others are downplayed.

The book argues that propaganda strategy depends in part on the relationship between state policy and public opinion, showing how communication can create political room for either mobilization or restraint.
